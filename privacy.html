Zipline

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Short version: Zipline has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your files go directly from one of your devices to the other, encrypted end to end. The developer never receives your files, your file names, or your device names.

What Zipline does not collect

  • No account, email address, or phone number.
  • No usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking.
  • No copies of the files you send or receive, and no record of their names or sizes.

How a transfer works

Each installation of Zipline creates a random cryptographic identity that stays on your device. Devices connect to each other directly and everything they exchange is encrypted so that only the two devices can read it.

To help two devices find a route to each other across the internet, Zipline uses the public connection-assistance network run by n0, inc. (iroh). Those servers see your device's random identity and network addresses (such as its IP address), as any internet service does, in order to pass on connection attempts. When no direct route can be found, encrypted data may be relayed through them; they cannot decrypt it. Devices on the same local network discover each other with a local broadcast that does not leave that network.

What is stored on your device

Your device's identity key, the device name you chose, the list of devices you paired with, your settings, and the files you received. Removing the app removes this data (received files you moved elsewhere stay where you put them).

Children

Zipline does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
