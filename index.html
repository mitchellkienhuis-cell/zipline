Zipline

Zipline sends files straight from one of your devices to another: fast, encrypted, and without uploading to a cloud first.

Need help?

Email mitchellkienhuis@icloud.com and describe what happened, which devices you used, and what you expected. We usually answer within a few days.

Getting started

  • Install Zipline on both devices and open it on both.
  • On the same Wi‑Fi, devices appear under Devices by themselves. Allow local network access when asked.
  • Anywhere else: tap Add device on one device, copy its code, and paste it on the other. You only do this once.
  • Choose files, then tap the device to send to.

Common questions

The other device doesn't show up. Check that Zipline is open on both, that both are on the same Wi‑Fi, and that local network access is allowed in the system settings. If it still doesn't appear, pair with a code instead.

A transfer stopped when I left the app. Phones pause apps in the background. Open Zipline again and the transfer continues from where it stopped.

Where do received files go? On iPhone and iPad: the Files app, under On My iPhone › Zipline. On Mac: your Downloads folder. On Android: Downloads › Zipline.

"When they're ready" and "Later" didn't start. Zipline has to be open on the sending device at the moment the transfer starts.
